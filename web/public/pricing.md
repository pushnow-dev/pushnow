# Pricing - PushNow

Last updated: 2026-09-14

PushNow is an iOS notification inbox for AI agent results, automation outputs, monitoring alerts, and scheduled reports. Plans limit notification item volume; actual App Store availability, local currency, tax, billing period, and entitlement state must be confirmed in the app purchase sheet.

## Free

- Price: CNY 0 per month
- Limit: 50 notification items per day
- Best for: personal testing, light agent workflows, and validating device notification behavior
- Includes: email login, user-bound inbox, source keys, App push or in-app-only delivery controls, and dashboard access

## Plus

- Price: CNY 8 per month
- Limit: 500 notification items per day
- Best for: several automation workflows, higher notification volume, and regular monitoring summaries
- Includes: Free features plus increased daily quota

## Pro

- Price: CNY 18 per month
- Limit: unlimited daily notification items, subject to platform and abuse limits
- Best for: heavy workflows, frequent agent results, and broader automation coverage
- Includes: Plus features plus the highest available daily quota

## Purchase and Restore Notes

- The App Store purchase confirmation screen is the source of truth for final price and billing details.
- Use Restore Purchases if access is missing after reinstalling the app.
- RevenueCat entitlement state is used for premium access checks.
- Website pricing examples do not prove that a product is available in every App Store region.

## Delivery Notes

- API acceptance is not proof of a visible iPhone notification.
- Inbox storage, provider delivery, device permissions, Focus settings, and visible alerts are separate checks.
- Do not use PushNow as the only channel for emergency or safety-critical alerts.
